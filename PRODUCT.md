# PRODUCT.md

register: brand

## What this is

The personal brand site of Dr Scott Hannah, exercise physiologist. Two pages: a single-scroll homepage and a publications archive. The site is the product: its job is to make a visitor understand, within one scroll, who Scott is, what he measures, and why they should work with him.

## Users

- **The Googler arriving off-page**: recruiter, journalist, NHS commissioner, PhD applicant. Lands on /publications or a paper title from search. Judges authority by the research surface, not the hero.
- **The commercial buyer**: performance director or clinical lead deciding whether to engage him for consultancy. Needs something concrete to act on and a contact route that is not institutional.
- **The academic peer**: checks the publication record, follows the DOI, decides whether to collaborate.

## Brand

- **Voice words**: measured, kinetic, exact.
- **Core concept**: the body as measured data. A real baked run cycle rendered as a self-constructing wireframe is the hero and the brand mark. Every downstream motif (tabular numerals, teal data-ink, hairline measurement rules, the teal full stop) extends that concept.
- **Identity is committed**: Switzer (one family, four weights), slate off-white paper, slate near-black ink, one teal accent used as data ink. These are settled choices; do not re-litigate them.
- **Positioning**: whole-body, systems-level physiology. Basic and applied science valued equally. Exercise as medicine. No institution framing: Scott, not his employer, is the brand.

## Tone and copy rules

- First person, declarative, plain-language findings over formal titles.
- No em dashes anywhere in front-facing copy. No emojis, ever; icons come from an SVG set.
- Every word earns its place. No restated headings.

## Anti-references

- Generic academic homepage (headshot, h-index widget, bullet CV).
- SaaS template minimalism: hero-metric blocks, identical icon-card grids, pill buttons.
- "AI-made" tells of any kind. The bar is a visitor asking "how was this made?"

## Strategic principles

1. The hero's concept must carry through the whole page, not stop at the fold.
2. The publications archive is the proof engine, not an appendix.
3. Restraint at the token level, ambition at the concept level.
